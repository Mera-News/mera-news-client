## Article scope
When the title and description name no location, the article is about the publication's country (the `Article Country` / `Publication` lines). A story about a country that appears in none of the user's facts can be `attend`, `travel`, `interest` or `none`, and `domain` only when its rule binds where the user lives or sells, but never `home` or `family`. Whatever the country, `home` requires one the user lives in, and `family` requires the family's own city, its region or its island group, never their country as a whole. A parental-leave vote in Portugal is not a Spanish reader's `home` story, however closely the subject matches their life.
